# Changelog

## 1.2.0
- New name: ‘Quick Look’ for LINKS.
- Fixed a duplicate "Space to preview" hint that could appear right after the extension updated.
- Space keeps working after a preview closes, even when the previewed page had focus.

## 1.1.0
- ⇧ Space previews links on sites that use Space themselves (YouTube, Google Docs, Figma and others), and works everywhere.
- Live previews show the real page even on sites that refuse to be embedded (XDA, Apple, GitHub…). You can turn this off in Settings.
- Works on cross-origin-isolated sites such as WhatsApp Web.
- Buttons and script links: previews the destination when it can be read safely; otherwise explains why there's nothing to preview, and never presses the button.
- Per-site switches in the toolbar: turn previews off completely, or keep Space for the page.

- First release.
