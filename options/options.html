      Privacy & safety

      • Nothing loads on hover. A page is fetched only after you press the shortcut.
      • Logged out by default. Previews load without your cookies, in a frame with its own empty cookie jar, so a preview can’t act on your accounts.
      • Sealed off. Previews render inside a locked-down extension frame. Their scripts can’t touch the page you’re on, and a previewed page can’t redirect your tab.
      • No servers, no analytics. The only request is the one to the link you chose. Your stats below never leave this computer.
      Refuse risky linksSkips links that look like sign-out, unsubscribe, delete or one-time sign-in links, which some sites act on with a single visit. You can still choose “Preview Anyway”.
      Explain buttonsWhen Space lands on a button or a script link with no page behind it, a short note says why there’s nothing to preview, instead of scrolling. Buttons are never pressed.

            About

            ‘Quick Look’ for LINKS brings Finder’s Quick Look to links: hover, press Space, flip through with the arrow keys. It works in Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers.
