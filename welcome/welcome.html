Keys
Privacy
  • Nothing loads on hover. Only when you press Space.
  • Logged out by default. A preview can’t act on your accounts.
  • Sealed off. Previewed pages can’t touch the page you’re on, or redirect your tab.
  • Careful with risky links. Sign-out, unsubscribe and delete links aren’t loaded.
  • No servers, no analytics. Nothing leaves your computer except the request to the link you chose.
Settings Space keeps its normal job in text boxes, editors, players and games. On sites like YouTube and Google Docs, where Space is taken, use ⇧ Space.
